Privacy Policy

This English version is a non-binding translation. Only the German version is legally binding; in case of any discrepancy, the German version prevails.

Motion Beats is an app that simulates engine sounds based on your movement. This Privacy Policy explains how Motion Beats handles your information. It covers both the app and this website; some sections apply to one of them only.

1. No Data Collection

Motion Beats does not collect, store, or share any personal data through the app.

  • The app does not access your contacts, identity, email, or any external accounts.
  • No user accounts, analytics, or third-party data collection services are used.
  • All processing happens entirely on your device.

2. Motion and Location Sensors

The app uses motion and location sensors solely to simulate engine sound based on your speed and acceleration, in real time while the app is running. All sensor data is processed locally on the device and is never transmitted or stored externally. We do not receive this data and are not the responsible party for the processing that takes place on your device.

3. Website

This website is static. We do not use forms, analytics, or cookies ourselves. It is hosted by GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages). When you open a page, the hosting provider may process technical data such as your IP address in order to deliver and secure the site, possibly outside the EU/EEA, for example in the United States. We do not analyze or use this data. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the secure and stable delivery of the site). For details, see GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

4. In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple Inc. Payment data is processed by Apple in accordance with Apple’s privacy policy; Apple is solely responsible for this data.

5. Email Correspondence

If you contact us by email, we use the data you provide (your email address and the content of your message) only to handle your request. We do not use it for any other purpose and do not pass it on to third parties. Our emails are handled by our email provider, Apple (iCloud Mail), which processes them technically to provide the email service. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in handling inquiries). We store your email as long as it is needed to handle your request. After that, we delete it, unless we are legally required to keep it.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The current version is always available at tseylerd.github.io/motion-beats-privacy-policy.

Last updated: November 14, 2026

7. Contact

Motion Beats is operated by the person named in the Imprint (Impressum). If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact us using the contact details given there.

For the processing described in sections 3 and 5 (website and email), the responsible party is the person named in the Imprint, and you have the rights under Art. 15–21 GDPR.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint directly with a supervisory authority at any time, in particular in the EU member state of your habitual residence, place of work, or the place of the alleged infringement.

Motion Beats erhebt, speichert und teilt keinerlei personenbezogene Daten über die App.

  • Die App greift nicht auf Ihre Kontakte, Ihre Identität, Ihre E-Mail-Adresse oder externe Konten zu.

Die App verwendet Bewegungs- und Ortungssensoren ausschließlich, um während der Laufzeit der App in Echtzeit Motorengeräusche basierend auf Ihrer Geschwindigkeit und Beschleunigung zu simulieren. Alle Sensordaten werden lokal auf dem Gerät verarbeitet und niemals übertragen oder extern gespeichert. Wir erhalten diese Daten nicht und sind nicht die verantwortliche Stelle für die Verarbeitung, die auf Ihrem Gerät stattfindet.
